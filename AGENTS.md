# Project Guidelines & Business Rules - Appliance Repair Knights

## 🚨 Mandatory Content & Pricing Constraints (STRICT RULE)

Across the **entire project** (including all blogs, service pages, location pages, meta tags, schema markup, and AI/LLM files like `llms.txt`):

### 1. No Specific Pricing / Cost Numbers
- **DO NOT** mention specific dollar amounts, price ranges, or cost numbers (e.g., avoid "$150", "$280 CAD", "$350", or exact hourly rates).
- **INSTEAD USE:**
  - *"Transparent, upfront written quotes before any repair begins."*
  - *"Diagnostic service call fee is completely waived when you proceed with the repair."*
  - *"Competitive local pricing with no hidden charges."*

### 2. No Specific Warranty Duration / Day Numbers
- **DO NOT** mention specific numbers for warranty days, months, or years (e.g., avoid "90-day warranty", "30 days", "1-year", etc.).
- **INSTEAD USE:**
  - *"Comprehensive written warranty on parts and labour."*
  - *"Fully guaranteed repairs with genuine OEM parts."*
  - *"Backed by our solid parts and labour protection."*

### 3. No Hardcoded Operational Day Counts
- **DO NOT** hardcode numerical day counts (e.g., avoid "7 days a week" or numeric day guarantees).
- **INSTEAD USE:**
  - *"Available daily for prompt dispatch and emergency diagnostics."*
  - *"Flexible same-day appointment scheduling across Toronto and the GTA."*

### 4. No PPC / Paid Landing Page Folders in SEO or LLM Files
- **DO NOT** link or include PPC campaign folders (such as `/same-day-repair/`) in `llms.txt`, XML sitemaps, internal organic breadcrumbs, or AI indexing files. Keep paid traffic landing pages completely separate from organic SEO and LLM discoverability.
