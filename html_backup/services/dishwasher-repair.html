Dishwasher Performance Inspection & Repairs

Dishwashers utilize water pressure, drainage loops, and heating elements. If a float switch sticks, a motor seal breaks, or the main drain pump fails, it can cause severe flooding. Our local technicians inspect and fix all common faults using original manufacturer parts.

Common Dishwasher Problems We Fix

  • Not Draining: Food particle clogs in the sump filter basin, or failed drain pumps.
  • Leaking: Gaps in door perimeter gaskets, cracked spray arms, or split pump casings.
  • Poor Washing Results: Clogged spray arm nozzles or faulty water inlet valves.
  • Keypad Dead: Control board failures or door latch micro-switch issues.
